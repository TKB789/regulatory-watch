Scope: Live updates from key regulatory markets that publish public RSS / Atom feeds — FDA, EU Commission, MHRA, Health Canada, TGA, plus the Federal Register and IMDRF. Other markets (Japan, China, India, Brazil, S. Korea) are listed in the Agencies tab with direct links to their official sites.

Each entry below shows whether that authority is monitored automatically (●) or only manually-listed in the Agencies tab (○).

  • FDA — recalls, safety, MedWatch, press
  • Federal Register — FDA pre-publications
  • AccessGUDID — UDI database
  • EMA / EU Commission — health news
  • MHRA — UK news, drug & device alerts
  • Health Canada — recalls & safety
  • TGA — Australia news & alerts
  • IMDRF — international harmonization
  • PMDA (Japan) — no public feed
  • NMPA (China) — no public feed
  • CDSCO (India) — no public feed
  • ANVISA (Brazil) — no public feed
  • MFDS (S. Korea) — no public feed
  • Swissmedic — email newsletter only
  • SFDA (Saudi Arabia) — no usable feed

For untracked authorities, use the Agencies tab to jump directly to each regulator's official page, or use "Search online for additional info" on any item.
